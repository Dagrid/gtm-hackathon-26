# ICP v1: AI-Assisted Development Setup (Ireland Beachhead)

## Confidence
LOW / HYPOTHESIS. Built from founder inputs and reasoning only, no interviews or market signals tested yet. Working draft to sharpen once outbound is live.

## Firmographic Profile
- Location: Ireland, specifically where engineering tooling and process decisions are made locally. Covers Irish-headquartered companies, and Ireland sites of foreign companies only if that site owns its own tooling decisions rather than executing a global mandate.
- How to spot local authority: the person posting the role or asking the question is Ireland-based with a senior enough title (Head of Engineering, CTO, Director of Engineering). If a global TA team or a non-Ireland office is coordinating it, treat as uncertain, not qualified.
- Dev model: in-house engineering team, or hybrid (in-house plus some outsourcing).
- Size: 20+ employees total. No upper ceiling: a large company is fine as long as the Ireland site decides its own tooling.

## Disqualifiers
- No engineering team at all.
- Fewer than 20 employees total.
- Engineering tooling and process decisions are made outside Ireland, with no local authority.
- Development is mostly outsourced to 3rd-party agencies (real segment, but a different buyer and a different pitch, out of scope for this round, worth its own v2 ICP).

## Buying Triggers
Primary: job postings or public queries about AI-assisted development process expertise (roles or briefs mentioning Copilot/Cursor rollout, AI coding workflow design, developer productivity or AI tooling leads). Directly detectable through job-board and Apollo job-postings data, so this is the one to build the first outbound list on.

Secondary (ideas, not yet tested, expand and cut based on real reply data once outbound runs):
- New VP Engineering, CTO, or Head of Engineering hire at the Ireland site. New leaders commonly re-evaluate tooling and vendors in their first 90 to 100 days, a widely-cited pattern in B2B sales, but not yet tested for this specific offer.
- Public posts on LinkedIn or engineering blogs about adopting or struggling with AI coding tools. Real signal, but needs social or content monitoring, not something Apollo surfaces on its own.

## Likely Buyer (hypothesis)
Champion: Head of Engineering, Engineering Manager, or CTO at the Ireland site.
Budget owner: CTO or COO for companies nearer the 20-100 headcount range; Head of Engineering with VP or CTO sign-off for larger ones.
Not tested. First few real conversations should confirm or correct this.

## Anti-Persona Signals
- No engineering team, or fewer than 20 employees.
- Ireland site with no local tooling authority, decision already made elsewhere.
- Development is mostly outsourced to 3rd-party agencies.
- Company already running a centrally-driven AI-adoption program the Ireland site didn't choose and can't change.

## Deliberately left out
Jobs-to-be-done, validated pain points, exact language, and where-they-hang-out sections aren't in this draft. All of those need real conversations to fill in honestly; guessing at them here would just be fiction with a template around it. Fill in after the first 5 to 10 real replies.
